Questions

Good to know.

How far ahead should we book?

Weddings 6–9 months, corporate 6–10 weeks, private parties 4+ weeks. Short on time? Ask — we often can.

How does payment work?

A 30% deposit secures your date, then milestone payments tied to the plan. Everything is itemised.

Can you work with our venue?

Yes. We handle your venue or recommend vetted ones across Kenya and the region.

What if plans change?

Date and scope changes are covered in your agreement, with clear terms up front.
